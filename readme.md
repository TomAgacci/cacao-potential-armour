INSTRUCTIONS WITH MEASUREMENTS & TIMING
---------------------------------------

1. MATERIALS (FOR 1 KG BATCH)
   - 400 g hydrated lime
   - 400 g fine silica sand
   - 120 g hemp hurds
   - 60 g cacao resin
   - 20 g defatted hemp protein powder
   - 45–65 mL water
   - 4–10 g salt dissolved into water

2. MIX RATIOS
   - Lime: 40%
   - Sand: 40%
   - Hemp hurds: 12%
   - Cacao resin: 6%
   - Hemp protein: 2%
   - Water: 4.5–6.5%
   - Salt: 1–2.5% of binder mass

3. WATER MANAGEMENT
   - Add water in 5 mL increments.
   - Mix 60 seconds after each addition.
   - Total water: 45–65 mL.

4. SALT MANAGEMENT
   - Dissolve salt fully into water.
   - Stir brine 30–45 seconds.
   - Add brine over 2–3 minutes.

5. MIXING STEPS
   - Lime + sand: 90 seconds
   - Add hurds: 60–90 seconds
   - Add resin + protein: 2 minutes
   - Add brine: 2–3 minutes
   - Final kneading: 1–2 minutes

6. COMPACTION
   - 20–30 kg force per layer (manual)
   - Or 1–3 MPa press pressure
   - Hold pressure 10–20 seconds per layer

7. CURING
   - Humid cure: 24–48 hours at 70–90% humidity
   - Air carbonation: 7–21 days with airflow

8. OUTPUT
   - Theoretical HSN strength band: 3–5 MPa
