HSN FULL‑OPTIMIZED 3–5 MPa MATERIAL — MEASURED PROCEDURAL INSTRUCTIONS
=====================================================================

1. MATERIAL SETUP (MEASURED)
----------------------------
For a 1 kg batch (easy scaling):
- Hydrated Lime: 400 g
- Fine Silica Sand: 400 g
- Hemp Hurds (medium-fine): 120 g
- Cacao Resin (melted or dissolved): 60 g
- Defatted Hemp Protein Powder: 20 g
- Water: 45–65 mL (target low-water ratio)
- Salt (NaCl): 4–10 g dissolved into water (brine)

2. TARGET RATIOS (BY MASS)
--------------------------
- Lime: 40%
- Sand: 40%
- Hemp Hurds: 12%
- Cacao Resin: 6%
- Hemp Protein: 2%
- Water: 4.5–6.5% (by total mass)
- Salt: 1–2.5% of binder mass (lime + resin)

3. WATER CONTROL (TIMED)
------------------------
- Add water in 5 mL increments.
- Mix for 60 seconds after each addition.
- Stop when mix is stiff, packable, and holds shape when squeezed.
- Total mixing time: 4–6 minutes.

4. SALT CONTROL (TIMED)
-----------------------
- Dissolve salt fully into the water BEFORE adding.
- Stir brine for 30–45 seconds until clear.
- Add brine gradually over 2–3 minutes during mixing.

5. MIXING PROCEDURE (TIMED)
---------------------------
- Step 1: Combine lime + sand for 90 seconds.
- Step 2: Add hemp hurds; mix 60–90 seconds.
- Step 3: Add cacao resin + hemp protein; mix 2 minutes.
- Step 4: Add brine slowly over 2–3 minutes.
- Step 5: Final mix kneading: 1–2 minutes.
- Total mixing time: 7–10 minutes.

6. COMPACTION (MEASURED)
------------------------
- Fill molds in 3 layers.
- Compact each layer with 20–30 kg of force (manual) or 1–2 MPa press pressure.
- Final top-layer compression: 30–45 kg force or 2–3 MPa press pressure.
- Hold pressure for 10–20 seconds per layer.

7. CURING (TIMED)
-----------------
Stage 1 — Humid Cure:
- Duration: 24–48 hours
- Humidity: 70–90%
- Temperature: 18–24°C
- Keep covered or in a humidity chamber.

Stage 2 — Air Carbonation:
- Duration: 7–21 days
- Airflow: moderate
- CO₂ exposure: normal ambient
- Temperature: 18–26°C

8. EXPECTED PERFORMANCE
-----------------------
When all parameters are optimized:
- Theoretical compressive strength: 3–5 MPa
- Strongest results occur with:
  - Low water
  - Low salt
  - High compaction
  - Full carbonation

END OF INSTRUCTIONS
